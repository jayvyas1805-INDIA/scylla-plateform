# About Scylla

Scylla is a platform for motorsport racing teams and the vendors who
supply them.

Teams register on Scylla and, once approved by an admin, get a public
profile listing their members, vehicles, achievements, sponsors, and
photo gallery. Team admins can add members, who each get their own
login to manage their own certificates and profile details.

Vendors are businesses that supply parts, services, or equipment to
racing teams. Vendors register, get approved by an admin, and then get
a public profile listing their services, past projects, business
hours, and a gallery. Vendors and teams can list products in a shared
marketplace and message each other directly about a listing.

Admins review and approve/reject team and vendor registrations, review
verification documents, moderate marketplace products, and manage
public content shown on the landing page.

Scylla is not just a single racing team's website — it's a shared
platform hosting many independent teams and vendors, each with their
own public profile.
